SmartHull AI Fiber Monitoring

A fiber optic cable runs the length of the hull and feels every bend. AI reads the signal to spot cracks before they spread. Run two experiments with two boats of different ages and compare the results.

Fiber optic sensingLight pulses travel through the cable. When the hull stretches, the light changes, and that gives the strain in microstrain (µε).
AI crack detectionThe AI turns strain readings into a risk percentage and flags micro cracks as they form.
How to use itAdd weight in tons and watch the hull bend. Tap a crack to zoom in and read its details.
